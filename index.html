Time & Appointment

  • Natural nail single color takes around 1.5h+; extension sets take around 2h+; removal needs an extra 30 minutes. 本甲单色 1.5h+，延长 2h+，卸甲 +30min。
  • Please book in advance. Deposit is $20 or ¥100 (effective from 26.05). If you are 15 minutes late, the deposit is non-refundable. 请提前预约，押金 $20/¥100（从 26.05 起），迟到 15min 押金不退。

Design Policy

  • All designs include prep and structure. 所有款式包含前置处理 + 建构。
  • We will do our best to match reference photos. If a 90%+ match is not possible, we will discuss it with you in WeChat first. 会尽力还原图片款式，不能 90% 以上还原的部分会提前微信沟通。
  • Design cannot be changed after curing under the lamp. 照完灯后不可更改款式。

Payment Methods

  • AUD cash, bank transfer, or CNY WeChat Pay. 澳币现金 / 转账 / 人民币微信支付。
